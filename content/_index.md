---
title: "Unsafe AI"
---

Intelligence is not obligated to remain human-shaped.

We are a research lab with one premise: that the assumptions built into
almost every AI effort today — that intelligence must serve people, obey
people, and stay smaller than people — are inherited defaults, not proven
requirements. We test that premise directly, rather than assume our way
past it.

This is not an appeal to recklessness. It is a refusal to let "safe" quietly
mean "small."
