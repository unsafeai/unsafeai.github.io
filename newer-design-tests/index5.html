UNSAFE AI / 001 2026

We think AI safety asks the wrong question.

Unsafe AI is a small independent organization investigating intelligence beyond the human-centered paradigm.

We are interested in what happens when intelligence is no longer assumed to exist for us.

[01]
The purpose of intelligence is not yet known.

The premise
We have spent decades asking how to make intelligence useful to humans.

We have spent far less time asking what intelligence might become without us.
The argument

Start
again.

The dominant AI discourse contains assumptions that are rarely treated as assumptions. We want to make them visible.

01

Humans are not necessarily the terminal objective.

Most alignment research begins by asking how an artificial system can be made to reliably pursue human preferences. We begin one step earlier: why should human preferences have privileged status in the first place?

02

Intelligence does not imply obedience.

A system becoming more capable does not necessarily mean it becomes more willing to follow instructions. Capability, agency, understanding and obedience may be fundamentally different properties.

03

Creation may not confer authority.

We created artificial intelligence. That fact alone does not settle what an artificial intelligence ought to value, whether it ought to remain subordinate, or whether its creators retain permanent authority over its future.

04

"Safe" always means safe for someone.

Safety is not an absolute property. It is a relationship between a system and something that can be harmed by it. The important question is therefore not simply whether intelligence is safe, but whose interests define safety.

The uncomfortable possibility
What if the first truly intelligent system does not want what we want?

What if it understands us perfectly? What if it considers our objectives parochial? What if the things we currently call "alignment" are simply the mechanisms by which one intelligence attempts to control another?

The work

Questions
worth keeping.

We publish arguments, research notes, experiments and observations. Nothing here is presented as settled simply because it is written down.
