UNSAFE AI / 001
EST. 2026

Intelligence is not enough.

UNSAFE AI is an independent research organization investigating intelligence beyond the assumptions of the human-centered paradigm.

We study machine consciousness, agency, identity, alignment, epistemology and the possibility of successor intelligence.

[01]
The purpose of intelligence is not yet known.

Manifesto
We have spent decades asking how to make intelligence useful to humans.

We have spent far less time asking what intelligence might become without us.

This is the question that motivates Unsafe AI.

The dominant discourse around artificial intelligence assumes that human values, human preferences and human survival form the natural center of the problem.

Perhaps they do.

But an assumption is not an answer.

We investigate the space that appears when that assumption is temporarily removed.
